Our story

It started with one very stubborn hat.

Crochet Curio began at a kitchen table in 2021, with a half-finished bucket hat and a lot of unpicking. What was meant to be a winter project turned into a waiting list, and the waiting list turned into this small studio.

We still work the same way: one maker, one piece at a time, in yarn we would happily wear ourselves. Nothing is mass produced, and nothing is rushed — a bag takes about nine hours, a bralette closer to five.

If a stitch is wrong, it comes out. That is the whole business model.

How it is made

Three steps, no shortcuts

  1. Yarn is chosen

    Undyed cotton, British merino and alpaca, bought in small lots from two suppliers we have used since the beginning.

  2. The piece is worked

    Every stitch by hand, start to finish, by one maker. No panels are outsourced and no seams are machine-joined.

  3. It is blocked and checked

    Washed, pinned, dried flat, then checked against the original sample before it is wrapped and sent.

Know when a restock lands

Pieces sell out in small numbers and come back slowly. One email when something new is listed — nothing else.
